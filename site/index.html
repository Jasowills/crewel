How it works

One prompt to the lead. The crew does the rest — isolated, live, review-gated.

Single-entry

crewel init once. Then crewel with no args launches the 6-pane UI with the lead focused. You type once; the lead fans out.

crewel init → crewel → prompt lead

◈

1 — Init

Pick team size, agents and how many, and the orchestrator. Writes crewel.json.

⬢

2 — Launch

crewel spawns 6 real PTYs (ConPTY on Windows, forkpty on macOS) — lead 58% left, 2+2+1 grid right.

✦

3 — Prompt the lead

Lead decomposes into tickets, assigns round-robin, handles needs-clarification, reviews, and is the only one who merges to main.

Isolated worktrees

Each teammate on crewel/{team}/integration + per-teammate branches. Main untouched until close-out.

◎

Live, not polled

team watch streams board and mail via fs.watch.

◐

Resilient

Interrupt, stall watchdog, rate-limit pause, freeze after 3 failures. Worktrees never auto-deleted.
